What to Expect When Working With Me

When you begin counseling with me, you can expect a thoughtful, collaborative process focused on insight, self-understanding, and meaningful change. Our work is grounded in the belief that lasting growth comes from understanding why you think, feel, and respond the way you do—not just managing symptoms.

We will explore your life story, including formative relationships and experiences that have shaped your identity, patterns, and emotional responses. My approach is insight-oriented and informed by psychodynamic principles, with a strong emphasis on person-centered work. I also use gentle Socratic questioning and CBT-based strategies to help you identify and reframe unhelpful thought patterns when they are getting in your way.

Sessions are guided by what is most important to you. Each week, I’ll ask how you’re really doing—not just what happened, but how you’re feeling, coping, and applying insights from our work together. Rather than following a rigid agenda, we focus on the themes that naturally emerge, trusting that the most important issues will surface when given space.

You won’t be overloaded with generic or busy-work homework. When between-session practices are suggested, they are purposeful, practical, and tailored to your goals. Much of the work happens through reflection, increased awareness, and learning to respond to yourself and others with greater clarity and intention.

At the core of my work is the belief that you already possess the wisdom needed to move forward. At times, that wisdom can be obscured by ego defenses, old survival strategies, or cognitive distortions. My role is to help you recognize and clear those obstacles so you can live more authentically and engage in healthier, more satisfying relationships.

Counseling with me is not about quick fixes. It is a process of understanding yourself more fully and creating sustainable change—one session at a time.

Our Services

Individual Counseling

Individual counseling begins with a thorough intake, clinical formulation, and individualized treatment plan. Sessions are typically held weekly and are tailored to the client’s goals, strengths, and challenges.

Treatment is Person-Centered in nature and integrates evidence-based approaches such as Psychodynamic Therapy, CBT, MI, DBT, and REBT. The goal is to provide a safe, supportive, and structured space where clients can gain insight, build skills, and create lasting change in their lives.

Couples Counseling

Couples counseling begins with a comprehensive relationship assessment and collaborative treatment plan designed to identify patterns of conflict, communication breakdowns, and emotional disconnection. Evidence-based tools such as Prepare/Enrich are used to assess relationship strengths, growth areas, and compatibility factors.

Treatment is informed by Gottman Method principles, with a focus on improving communication, managing conflict, strengthening emotional connection, and increasing mutual understanding. Therapy is also increasingly guided by Imago Relationship Therapy, which helps couples understand how early relational experiences influence adult partnership dynamics. Couples are supported in developing healthier interaction patterns, repairing trust, and building a more secure and intentional relationship.

Group Counseling

Group counseling provides a structured, therapist-led environment where individuals can develop insight, skills, and accountability alongside others facing similar challenges. Groups are intentionally designed to balance psychoeducation, interpersonal process, and practical skill-building.

Participants benefit not only from professional guidance, but also from shared experience, feedback, and normalization that occurs within the group setting. Group counseling is particularly effective for addressing issues such as emotional regulation, relationships, stress, anger, and personal growth. The group format fosters connection, perspective-taking, and real-world application of therapeutic skills, helping participants move from insight to meaningful, sustainable change.

M.E.N.S. program

Mood, Anxiety, Stress, ADHD, and Anger

Men often experience depression, anxiety, symptoms of ADHD, and stress through irritability, emotional shutdown, overthinking, or anger. This focus area addresses mood disorders, anxiety management, ADHD management techniques, stress reduction, and anger regulation using evidence-based approaches. Treatment helps men improve emotional control, cognitive flexibility, and behavioral responses while maintaining strength, clarity, and performance.

Emerging Adulthood & Midlife Transitions

Emerging adulthood and midlife transitions can trigger identity confusion, anxiety, depression, and relational strain. This work supports men navigating career uncertainty, role changes, regret, or loss of direction. Therapy focuses on identity development, purpose, values clarification, and adaptive decision-making to support psychological growth and long-term well-being.

Navigating Relationships

Difficulties with communication, emotional intimacy, boundaries, and conflict are common concerns in men’s therapy. This focus area helps men improve romantic, family, and interpersonal relationships by developing emotional awareness, accountability, and effective communication skills. Treatment supports healthier attachment patterns, conflict resolution, and relationship repair.

Substance Abuse & Addictive Behaviors

Substance use and addictive behaviors often function as coping strategies for stress, anxiety, or emotional distress. This area addresses alcohol use, drug use, and behavioral addictions through evidence-based treatment focused on behavior change, emotional regulation, relapse prevention, and healthier coping skills that support long-term recovery.
