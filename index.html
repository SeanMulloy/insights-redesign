About Me

I am an experienced, effective professional treating a broad range of psychiatric issues and conditions. I primarily do Individual Counseling, Couples Counseling, and Group Counseling. Grounded in the belief that people inherently possess the knowledge and ability needed to overcome most of life's challenges, most need help to break through defenses and blind spots that can obscure one’s perspective. I especially enjoy working with young adult males and I have substantial experience working with emerging adulthood issues. I specialize in working with adult men, but I also do very well with adult women, couples and group counseling settings. I create a safe, comfortable, non-judgmental environment where clients can do life-changing work.

Our Services

Individual Counseling

Individual counseling begins with a thorough intake, clinical formulation, and individualized treatment plan. Sessions are typically held weekly and are tailored to the client’s goals, strengths, and challenges. Treatment is Person-Centered in nature and integrates evidence-based approaches such as Psychodynamic Therapy, CBT, MI, DBT, and REBT. The goal is to provide a safe, supportive, and structured space where clients can gain insight, build skills, and create lasting change in their lives.

Couples Counseling

Couples counseling begins with a comprehensive relationship assessment and collaborative treatment plan designed to identify patterns of conflict, communication breakdowns, and emotional disconnection. Evidence-based tools such as Prepare/Enrich are used to assess relationship strengths, growth areas, and compatibility factors. Treatment is informed by Gottman Method principles, with a focus on improving communication, managing conflict, strengthening emotional connection, and increasing mutual understanding. Therapy is also increasingly guided by Imago Relationship Therapy, which helps couples understand how early relational experiences influence adult partnership dynamics. Couples are supported in developing healthier interaction patterns, repairing trust, and building a more secure and intentional relationship.

Group Counseling

Group counseling provides a structured, therapist-led environment where individuals can develop insight, skills, and accountability alongside others facing similar challenges. Groups are intentionally designed to balance psychoeducation, interpersonal process, and practical skill-building. Participants benefit not only from professional guidance, but also from shared experience, feedback, and normalization that occurs within the group setting. Group counseling is particularly effective for addressing issues such as emotional regulation, relationships, stress, anger, and personal growth. The group format fosters connection, perspective-taking, and real-world application of therapeutic skills, helping participants move from insight to meaningful, sustainable change.

M.E.N.S. program

Mood, Anxiety, Stress, ADHD, and Anger

Men often experience depression, anxiety, symptoms of ADHD, and stress through irritability, emotional shutdown, overthinking, or anger. This focus area addresses mood disorders, anxiety management, ADHD management techniques, stress reduction, and anger regulation using evidence-based approaches. Treatment helps men improve emotional control, cognitive flexibility, and behavioral responses while maintaining strength, clarity, and performance.

Emerging Adulthood & Midlife Transitions

Emerging adulthood and midlife transitions can trigger identity confusion, anxiety, depression, and relational strain. This work supports men navigating career uncertainty, role changes, regret, or loss of direction. Therapy focuses on identity development, purpose, values clarification, and adaptive decision-making to support psychological growth and long-term well-being.

Navigating Relationships

Difficulties with communication, emotional intimacy, boundaries, and conflict are common concerns in men’s therapy. This focus area helps men improve romantic, family, and interpersonal relationships by developing emotional awareness, accountability, and effective communication skills. Treatment supports healthier attachment patterns, conflict resolution, and relationship repair.

Substance Abuse & Addictive Behaviors

Substance use and addictive behaviors often function as coping strategies for stress, anxiety, or emotional distress. This area addresses alcohol use, drug use, and behavioral addictions through evidence-based treatment focused on behavior change, emotional regulation, relapse prevention, and healthier coping skills that support long-term recovery.
