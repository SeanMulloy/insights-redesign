What to Expect When Working With Me

When you begin counseling with me, you can expect a thoughtful, collaborative process focused on insight, self-understanding, and meaningful change. Our work is grounded in the belief that lasting growth comes from understanding why you think, feel, and respond the way you do-not just managing symptoms.

We will explore your life story, including formative relationships and experiences that have shaped your identity, patterns, and emotional responses. My approach is insight-oriented and informed by psychodynamic principles, with a strong emphasis on person-centered work. I also use gentle Socratic questioning and CBT-based strategies to help you identify and reframe unhelpful thought patterns when they are getting in your way.

Sessions are guided by what is most important to you. Each week, I’ll ask how you’re really doing-not just what happened, but how you’re feeling, coping, and applying insights from our work together. Rather than following a rigid agenda, we focus on the themes that naturally emerge, trusting that the most important issues will surface when given space.

You won’t be overloaded with generic or busy-work homework. When between-session practices are suggested, they are purposeful, practical, and tailored to your goals. Much of the work happens through reflection, increased awareness, and learning to respond to yourself and others with greater clarity and intention.

At the core of my work is the belief that you already possess the wisdom needed to move forward. At times, that wisdom can be obscured by ego defenses, old survival strategies, or cognitive distortions. My role is to help you recognize and clear those obstacles so you can live more authentically and engage in healthier, more satisfying relationships.

Counseling with me is not about quick fixes. It is a process of understanding yourself more fully and creating sustainable change-one session at a time.
