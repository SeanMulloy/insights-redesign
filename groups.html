Group Counseling

Group counseling provides a structured, therapist-led environment where individuals can develop insight, skills, and accountability alongside others facing similar challenges. Groups are intentionally designed to balance psychoeducation, interpersonal process, and practical skill-building. Participants benefit not only from professional guidance, but also from shared experience, feedback, and normalization that occurs within the group setting. Group counseling is particularly effective for addressing issues such as emotional regulation, relationships, stress, anger, and personal growth. The group format fosters connection, perspective-taking, and real-world application of therapeutic skills, helping participants move from insight to meaningful, sustainable change.

ANGER MANAGEMENT GROUP

A structured, insight-driven group for men who struggle with anger, reactivity, and emotional control

This 12-week Anger Management group is designed for men who want to understand their anger—not just suppress it. Using a well-researched, skills-based workbook alongside guided group discussion, participants learn to identify triggers, recognize underlying emotions, and take responsibility for how anger shows up in their lives. Weekly Anger Logs help members slow reactions, increase awareness, and develop healthier responses to real-world situations.

Good fit for men who:

  • Feel easily triggered, reactive, or explosive
  • Struggle with frustration, irritability, or emotional buildup
  • Want tools that go beyond “just calm down”
  • Are seeking personal growth, court-recommended support, or both

MEN NAVIGATING RELATIONSHIPS

Understanding Yourself in Connection with Others

A process-oriented group for men who want stronger, healthier relationships.

Relationships are where many of life’s deepest challenges—and greatest opportunities for growth—show up. This group helps men better understand how they relate to partners, family members, friends, and colleagues. Through guided discussion, insight-building, and supportive feedback from other men, participants learn to recognize the patterns that shape their relationships and develop healthier ways of communicating, setting boundaries, and responding to conflict.

Rather than offering quick tips or surface-level advice, this group focuses on understanding the deeper dynamics that influence connection: emotional awareness, attachment patterns, communication habits, and unmet needs. The goal is to help men become more confident, intentional, and effective in the relationships that matter most.

Good fit for men who:

  • Struggle with communication or recurring conflict in relationships
  • Want to better understand their emotional reactions and triggers
  • Feel disconnected from partners, friends, or family
  • Want to develop stronger, more authentic connections with others
